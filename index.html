// WHAT VOUL IS

Built for footage, not just survival.

Voul SMP exists so creators have a world worth filming in. Bring a script and shoot a storyline, or show up and let the SMP write itself — both are the point.

Scripted — planned arcs, storylines, builds staged for a video
Unscripted — raw SMP chaos, clip it as it happens
Hybrid — loose plot, improvised execution
// SERVER RULES

How Voul runs.

Short version, four parts. Full detail below — read it before you apply.

01

General Conduct

Respect & Moderation
  • Be respectful — no hate speech, extreme toxicity, or targeted harassment.
  • Staff decisions are final. Take disputes to DMs or a ticket, not public chat.
02

Gameplay & Combat

PvP & Fair Play
  • PvP is enabled — fight freely, but no spawn-camping or relentless targeting.
  • Strict no-flooding policy: flooding bases or terrain with water, lava, or ice — or cobble monsters and obsidian griefs — is strictly banned.
  • No cheating. X-ray, hacked clients, and dupes are an instant ban. Sodium, Iris, and minimaps are allowed.
03

World & Bases

Building & Land Use
  • Base spacing: build at least 150+ blocks from neighbors unless mutually agreed otherwise.
  • Keep it clean — repair creeper holes, chop entire trees, remove pillar towers.
04

Content Creator Requirement

Monthly Quota
  • Because Voul is a creator server, members post at least once a month featuring server content.
Long-form — YouTube video
Live stream — Twitch, YouTube, or Kick
Short-form — YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Reels
